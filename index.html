NekoAI privacy policy

NekoAI is a cat companion app by Day Dream Corp. Last updated: 5 November 2026.

The short version

NekoAI has no account. Almost everything stays on your phone. When you use the AI features, what you say to the cat is sent to Anthropic, the AI provider. So that she can write you a short note when she misses you, a little about her is sent too, unless you turn her notes off, and nothing you said. While NekoAI is being tested, it also tells Day Dream Corp which features you use and how often, and what crashed, but never what you or the cat write or say; you can turn that off in Settings. Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else. If you choose to turn on accessibility mode, NekoAI finds where things are on your screen so the cat can walk on them, and that stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your conversation with the cat, the memories you let her keep, and the little notes she makes about what you like in small talk.
  • Her hunger, thirst, bond and habits (what you like doing with her), her personality, and your settings.
  • When she was last on your screen and when she last left you a note, used to space out her notes.
  • Your Anthropic API key, if you use your own, encrypted with Android's Keystore.
  • A record of your AI usage, used to keep the usage limits. Copies of NekoAI installed outside Google Play also keep a tiny usage-limit file in Documents/NekoAI so a reinstall doesn't reset the limits; the Google Play version doesn't.

None of this is sent to Day Dream Corp.

What leaves your phone, and why

  • To Anthropic, when you use the AI chat or small talk: what you write or say to the cat, pictures you choose to show her, and the context she needs to answer (her personality, the time and season, your usage percentages, and the notes and memories above). This is handled under Anthropic's privacy policy. Copies installed outside Google Play send it with your own API key. The Google Play version sends it through Neko Gate, Day Dream Corp's own relay, which holds the API key: Neko Gate checks with Google Play that the app is genuine, passes your messages on to Anthropic, and keeps only what it needs to run your plan (a random id for this install, your plan and its Google Play purchase, with what each order cost and what reached Day Dream Corp of it, whether the developer has blocked this install, and how much of your usage limits you have used), never the words. If you ask her about her settings, the ones she looks up go too, including any apps you've listed for her to avoid.
  • To Anthropic, for her notes, unless you turn them off: when she hasn't been on your screen for a day, she can leave you a notification, at most one a day, and the AI writes it. That sends her personality, her name, what she calls you and the language, the same way as the chat, and it counts against your usage. It doesn't send anything you wrote or said, your conversation, her memories or her notes about you. With no plan, no key or no usage left, nothing is sent and she uses a line of her own. Nothing is sent for a note you couldn't see: if notifications are off for NekoAI, or in Kid Mode, she doesn't write at all.
  • To Day Dream Corp, while NekoAI is being tested: anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, described below, unless you turn them off.
  • To Open-Meteo, only if you turn on Real weather: your approximate location, rounded to about 10 km, to get the current weather. See Open-Meteo's terms.
  • Voice input is turned into text by your phone's own speech recognizer. NekoAI never records or sends audio itself.

Everything is sent over encrypted connections.

Accessibility mode, only if you turn it on

NekoAI can use Android's accessibility service, so the cat can walk and sit on your apps. It stays off unless you do two things: tap I agree on the notice in the app (the Out tab, Accessibility mode), then turn it on yourself in Android's settings. If you don't, she stays on the ground in her small overlay window, if you've allowed that, and everything else keeps working.

What it reads, while it's on:

  • The position and size of things on your screen, like windows, buttons, cards and the keyboard, so she can walk and sit on them and step aside for full-screen video.
  • Which app is on screen, so she can keep away from the banking and password apps she recognizes, and any you list in Settings.

What it never does: it never reads what's on those things, like text, pictures or what you type, and it skips password fields altogether. It doesn't tap anything or act for you. The AI never receives any of this and can't use the service. It can only ask, with a tap from you, to change whether she walks on apps or which apps she avoids.

On Google Play it draws nothing: she stays in her small overlay window, which needs Android's Draw over other apps permission. Copies installed outside Google Play also use it to draw her on a layer over your apps, and your taps go through that layer, except on and right around her and her bubbles.

Where it goes: nowhere. It's used on your phone while the service runs, and none of it is saved or sent to anyone, Day Dream Corp and Anthropic included. While NekoAI is being tested, the usage statistics below say nothing about what it found. On Google Play they can't tell that it's on at all; from copies installed outside Google Play they can say that it went on or off.

Pictures of your screen: The Google Play version can't take pictures of your screen at all. Copies installed outside Google Play have one more feature, off unless you turn on Screen questions in Settings: when you tap My screen in the chat, the service takes one picture of the screen, never over banking or password apps. You see it before anything is sent, and if you send it, it goes to Anthropic like any other picture you show her.

Turn it off at any time in Android's accessibility settings, and she stays on the ground in the overlay window, if you've allowed it. Your yes stays on this phone, so turning it back on doesn't ask again, and it doesn't move to a new phone with your other data. Clearing NekoAI's storage, uninstalling, or closing that notice without agreeing removes it. Kid Mode can't ask for it. If a grown-up turned it on first, it stays on in Kid Mode and works the same way.

Usage statistics while NekoAI is being tested

Copies of NekoAI given to testers (the Google Play test track) can send Day Dream Corp's own server short reports of what the app is used for, so we can see which parts are loved and which ones break. The switch is Settings, Share anonymous usage with the developer. It starts on, but nothing is sent until you have said OK to the notice that explains it the first time you open the app.

What is sent is a list of things that happened, each a name from a fixed list and a few fixed words or numbers, never free text:

  • Opening the app (from the launcher, a notification, the share sheet, a tile, the widget or the cat), letting her out onto the screen and putting her away.
  • Tapping her, opening her action bubbles, and what you gave or did (which food, drink or treat, which kind of petting, toy, care or trick) with how she took it.
  • Small talk lines, and whether she answered with one of her own lines or with the AI.
  • Chat messages: only that one was sent, how (typed, by voice, shared from another app, or from a mini-app), its length as a rough size (up to 20, 80, 300 or 1,000 characters, or more), whether it was one of her little free commands, and the reasoning setting. Never the message itself, and never her answer.
  • Mini-apps made and opened (the name of the ready-made template, or "custom"), stamps earned, outfits worn, festivals shown, Kid Mode being turned on and off, and her antics you saw (a belly slam, a climb unless accessibility mode is on, clinging to the screen, tearing it).
  • Which of the steps of letting her out are allowed on your phone (drawing over apps, notifications, accessibility, being the assistant), when that changes, and which settings you changed: the setting's name, and what you set it to only for an on/off or a choice from a fixed list, never anything you typed.
  • The plan menu being opened, the plan you subscribed to (its name only, never a price, an order number or a purchase token), and the "new version" dialog.
  • Timings: how long her answers take, how long she takes to appear on the screen, and how long you spend on each of the app's screens.
  • Errors: the kind of error (for example "no network"), and for a crash, the type of error and the places in NekoAI's code where it happened (class and file names, line numbers). Never the error's message, which could quote something you typed.

Each report comes with a random id made on your phone the first time NekoAI runs (it isn't your name, an account, your phone number, the Android ID, an advertising id or a hardware serial number, and reinstalling the app makes a new one; on the Google Play version it is the same id Neko Gate knows this install by), your phone's model, Android version, the app's version and language, and your plan. Day Dream Corp's server doesn't keep your IP address.

Not sent, ever: what you or the cat write or say, your contacts, accounts, email, location, the names of other apps, or anything at all while Kid Mode is on (apart from the note that Kid Mode was turned on, and later off, so our server stops counting).

Only if you choose to, as a tester:

  • a tester name you pick, shown on the testers' leaderboard, in the app and in the NekoAI Discord server, and in a notification to other testers when you pull ahead;
  • linking this install to your Discord account, by typing a one-time code from the app in the NekoAI Discord;
  • sharing a picture of your cat in the #your-cat channel of the NekoAI Discord: a drawing the app makes of her (never a screenshot), her name and a short note you write. They are posted publicly, signed with your tester name, and deleted after 7 days.

Messages from the developer: while the switch is on, and never in Kid Mode, the app asks the same server whether the developer has written to testers, using that same random id, and shows what came as a notification, or as a card inside the app. The server notes which messages reached your install, and whether you saw and answered a card, and nothing else about them. Android lets you turn each kind of message down in its notification settings.

How long it is kept: the reports, 90 days; crash reports, 30 days; the daily counts the leaderboard reads, your tester name, your Discord link and which of the developer's messages reached you, until the install is erased; shared pictures, their names and notes, 7 days.

If an install is blocked: the developer can stop an install taking part. The server then takes nothing more from it, it leaves the leaderboard, and Neko Gate stops answering it, until the block is lifted. The server and Neko Gate keep that mark.

Your choices: turn the switch off at any time: nothing more is sent, and anything waiting to be sent is deleted from your phone. To have everything about your install erased from our server, write to us with your tester name or your Discord name.

Children and Kid Mode

NekoAI is for teens and adults. Kid Mode is a tool for parents, set up on their own phone: it shows only the cat, her action bubbles and gentle cat small talk, with no chat, no settings and no links, and it can only be turned off with the parent's fingerprint or face. The notes the cat makes in Kid Mode never keep names, addresses, schools, phone numbers or other identifying details, are kept apart from the adult's, and can be read and deleted by the parent in Neko Nikki. If AI small talk is used in Kid Mode, what the child says is sent to Anthropic as described above. She leaves no notes while Kid Mode is on. No usage statistics, tester name, Discord link, shared picture or crash report is sent while Kid Mode is on.

Permissions

  • Display over other apps, so the cat can live on your screen.
  • Microphone, only while you talk to her by voice.
  • Approximate location, only if you turn on Real weather.
  • Notifications, for her companion notification, mini-app reminders, her notes and, in test builds, news from the testers' leaderboard.
  • Run at startup, only so that the schedule for her notes survives restarting your phone. Nothing runs when the phone starts.
  • Fingerprint or face, to turn Kid Mode on and off (Android checks it; NekoAI never sees it).
  • Accessibility service, only if you agree and turn it on, as described above.

Your choices

You can forget any memory or note in Neko Nikki, turn memory, Real weather, her notes and the sharing of anonymous usage off in Settings, silence her notes in Android's notification settings, turn accessibility mode off in Android's settings, remove your API key, or delete everything on your phone by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. To have what our server received about your install erased, write to us.
